Provider case study

Continuity where the roster was hardest to sustain.

A regional aged-care home had relied on agency RNs for more than two years to cover a difficult night-duty line. Elite Nurse Partners supplied a full-time RN who stayed in the role for two years, completed the home’s local requirements and became familiar with its residents, systems and escalation pathways.

The result was a more consistent workforce arrangement, less repeated agency coordination and a practical alternative to ongoing agency dependence.
